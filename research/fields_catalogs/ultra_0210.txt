Care Instructions
Machine-washable in cold water, on delicate cycle, with like colors. 
Use mild detergent. Do not use bleach. We do not recommend 
color safe bleach. Tumble dry low or line. Can be steam ironed on 
low setting, with a press cloth. Please be aware that color 
migration may occur if dark-colored fabric is combined with white 
or light-colored fabric. Dry cleanable by conventional methods 
(except for white and pearlized).  Use #12 needles. 
See website for more details on each style.
