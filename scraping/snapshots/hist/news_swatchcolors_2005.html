Color Added to the Toray Ultrasuede Website

Ultrasuede® now has a more colorful website. Literally. As of Spring 2005, the Toray Ultrasuede website gives customers access to a new feature which allows them to view a full range of product colors and then request swatches for immediate delivery.

Until now, designers wanting to see a product's color range had to request mail delivery of a color card—and then follow up with yet another request for the color swatches that interested them.

"It was a time-saving decision on our part," comments Patty Munro, Manager of Corporate Communications with Toray Ultrasuede. "Now we've made it possible for designers and manufacturers to view colors on the spot and, even better, have an actual fabric sample in their hands within 24 hours."

"Saving even one day's time," she adds, "can greatly help our customers move their projects forward."

The new feature, which is currently available for apparel, interior design and automotive products, delivers approximately 250 solid colors as well as a select number of prints. Color views—and swatch requests—can be accessed via the Products and Applications pages of the site. Please look for "View Swatches" button in the section.
