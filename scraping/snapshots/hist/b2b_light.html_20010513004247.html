ULTRASUEDE® LIGHT:
LUSCIOUSLY INNOVATIVE

Only the originators of the world’s first ultra-microfiber could engineer a fabric so comfortably light and so elegantly drapable. Combining breezy light weight with luxurious breathability, Ultrasuede® Light is an ideal fabric for any garment and for every season.

Invitingly soft hand, sensuous feel
Plush suede surface
Resistance to crocking, pilling, stretching and shrinking
Heightened resistance to discoloration at perspiration points thanks to advanced dyeing techniques
Excellent colorfastness
Easy-care, machine-washable and dry-cleanable
40 high-fashion colors

SPECIFICATIONS
Style: 801
Composition: 100% polyester ultra-microfiber non-woven with non-fibrous polyurethane binder
Width: 58"
Thickness: 0.6mm
Weight: 5.0 oz. per square yard (±4%)
Fiber Fineness: 0.04d
